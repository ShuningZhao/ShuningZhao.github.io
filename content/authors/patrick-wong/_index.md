Patrick Wong is a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in the Department of Econometrics and Business Statistics at Monash Business School, Monash University.

His research focuses on actuarial science, financial mathematics, and commodity market dynamics. He specializes in the evaluation and valuation of variable annuities in general stochastic environments, modeling jump-diffusion processes in crude oil markets, and analyzing higher-order risk-neutral moments.
