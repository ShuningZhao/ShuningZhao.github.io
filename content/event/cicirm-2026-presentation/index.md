I am pleased to share that our paper, **"Diachronic Sample Integration: Robust Tail-Risk Estimation with Generative Models,"** has been accepted for presentation at the **16th China International Conference on Insurance and Risk Management (CICIRM 2026)**.

CICIRM 2026 will be held from **July 8 to 11, 2026**, at the **Yunnan Lianyun Hotel in Kunming, Yunnan, China**. The conference is organized by the China Center for Insurance and Risk Management at the School of Economics and Management, Tsinghua University, and co-organized by the School of Finance, Yunnan University of Finance and Economics.

I am grateful to my co-authors [Patrick Wong](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kceK35kAAAAJ&hl=en), **Leran Zhang**, and [Xiaolin Hu](https://www.xlhu.cn/), and I look forward to presenting our work and exchanging ideas with researchers and practitioners at CICIRM 2026.

More information about the conference is available on the official CICIRM website: <https://ccirm.sem.tsinghua.edu.cn/ccirm/index.html>.
